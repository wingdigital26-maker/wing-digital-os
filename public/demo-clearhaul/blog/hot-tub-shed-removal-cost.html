Hot tub, shed, and playset removal cost in DFW

By the Hero’s team · Updated July 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: in Dallas-Fort Worth, hot tub removal typically runs $300 to $600, shed teardown and haul-off $350 to $800, and playset removal $150 to $400. Access and material drive the ranges more than size does. (Figures are typical ranges for North Texas.)

These three jobs share a theme: they are the backyard items that outlive their welcome by about five years, because getting rid of them feels like a project. It is one, just not yours. Here is what each actually involves and what moves the price.

Hot tub removal: $300 - $600

A hot tub is 400 to 900 pounds of shell, frame, and waterlogged foam that does not fit through any gate in Texas. The crew disconnects nothing electrical (have an electrician kill and cap the circuit first), drains what is left, and then either walks it out on dollies or cuts it into sections with a reciprocating saw right in place. Cutting sounds dramatic; it is the standard method and takes about an hour.

  • Adds cost: second-story decks, tight side yards, built-in surrounds that need dismantling.
  • Saves cost: draining it the day before, clear path to the driveway, gate wider than 36 inches.

Shed teardown: $350 - $800

A typical 8x10 backyard shed comes down in a morning: contents cleared, panels unbolted or cut, frame dropped, everything loaded. Metal sheds are lighter but slower because of sharp panel handling; wood sheds are heavier but come apart faster. Roofing shingles on the shed add disposal weight, and a concrete pad is its own separate conversation.

  • Adds cost: full shed (clear it or have us quote the contents as part of the load), shingled roofs, sheds attached to fences or slabs.
  • Saves cost: emptying it first, unlocking the gate, flagging the sprinkler lines.

Playset and trampoline removal: $150 - $400

The swing set the kids outgrew is mostly bolts and stubborn lumber. Crews unbolt what unbolts, cut what does not, and separate the metal for recycling. Trampolines are lighter work and usually land at the bottom of the range. Solid wood playsets in genuinely good shape can sometimes be rehomed through neighborhood groups if you have the lead time; weathered ones are not safe to pass on.

Bundle the backyard: hot tub plus shed plus the pile behind the shed prices better as one truck visit than three separate calls. Volume pricing works in your favor; see how junk removal pricing works in DFW.

Frequently asked questions

How much does hot tub removal cost in DFW?

Typically $300 to $600 (typical ranges), driven by access and whether it needs to be cut into sections. Electrical disconnect by an electrician comes first.

How much does shed removal cost?

Typically $350 to $800 for a standard 8x10, depending on material, contents, and roofing. Quoted firm on sight before any work starts.

Do you need to drain a hot tub before removal?

Yes, ideally the day before. The crew can pump the last few inches but cannot safely move or cut a full tub.

Can a playset be donated instead of scrapped?

Good-condition wood sets sometimes find takers if disassembled with lead time. Weathered sets get recycled and disposed of instead.
