What junk removal costs in DFW (2026 guide)

By the Hero’s team · Updated July 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer: At Hero’s, hauling away a single item like a couch runs $75 to $150. A quarter trailer is $150 to $250, a half trailer $250 to $400, three-quarters $400 to $500, and a full trailer $500 to $600. What you pay comes down to how much of the trailer you fill. These are our prices, all in.

Nobody budgets for junk. It piles up quietly, then a move, a remodel, or an estate puts it on a deadline. This guide breaks down exactly how we price at Hero’s, so there are no surprises when we pull up.

These are all-in prices: crew, loading labor, disposal fees, and fuel. If a quote looks dramatically cheaper, one of those pieces is usually missing and shows up later as a surprise.

The five things that move the price

1. Volume

Our dump trailer is priced by the quarter: a single item, a quarter, a half, three-quarters, or a full load. The crew looks at your pile, tells you how much of the trailer it will fill, and the price follows. You can see the load line yourself, so it is the fairest system in the industry.

2. Weight

We price by volume, not by the pound. If a load turns out heavier than it looked once it is on the trailer, that is on us, not you. The number we quote is the number you pay, even when it weighs more than we expected.

3. Access and labor

A garage pile next to the driveway is the easy case. A sleeper sofa on the third floor, a hot tub behind a fence, or a long carry across a big backyard adds crew time. Good companies fold this into the upfront quote instead of springing it on you after loading.

4. Special disposal items

We take mattresses and electronics at no extra charge. Only a couple of items carry a small disposal fee, and we flag it before we load: refrigerators and freezers need freon recovery, and tires have a county surcharge in parts of DFW. You will always know the number upfront.

5. Legitimacy and accountability

The guy with a trailer on a neighborhood app is cheaper for a reason. No accountability if your door frame loses the fight, and no telling where the load actually ends up. Illegal dumping fines in Texas can land on the person who hired the hauler, so "where does this go?" is not a rude question. It is the smartest one you can ask.

Rule of thumb: count how many pickup-truck beds your pile would fill, then remember a full trailer holds about six of them. Describe that over the phone and any honest company can quote you within a range in two minutes. That is exactly how our phone estimates work.

Junk removal vs renting a dumpster

For a one-time cleanout, full-service junk removal usually wins once you do the real math. A 10-yard dumpster rental in DFW runs a few hundred dollars before the permit some cities require for street placement, and you still do every minute of the loading. A junk removal crew shows up, loads everything in an hour or two, and the driveway is clear the same day, no rusty box sitting out front for a week.

Dumpsters earn their keep on multi-week renovations where debris shows up daily. If a contractor is producing scrap for a month, rent the box. If the junk already exists and you want it gone, book the crew.

Questions to ask before you book any hauler

  • Is the quote all-in, or are disposal fees and labor extra?
  • Is the crew employed, or day labor hired just for your job?
  • What happens to donatable items, and can I get a donation receipt?
  • Where does the load actually get disposed of?
  • Is the price confirmed before loading starts?

Any reputable company can answer all five without flinching. Hero’s quotes every job upfront, in writing if you want it, and the quote is the bill.

Frequently asked questions

How much does junk removal cost in DFW?

At Hero’s, single items run $75 to $150 and a full trailer $500 to $600. Those are our prices, and how much of the trailer you fill sets the number.

Why do junk removal quotes vary so much?

Volume, weight, labor factors like stairs and long carries, special disposal fees, and whether the company actually stands behind the work. Compare quotes on what is included, not just the bottom line.

Is junk removal cheaper than renting a dumpster?

For one-time cleanouts, usually yes once you count the rental, permit, and your own loading time. Dumpsters win for long renovations producing debris daily.

What items cost extra to dispose of?

Very few. We take mattresses and electronics at no extra charge. Only refrigerators and freezers (freon recovery) and tires carry a small disposal fee, and we flag it before we load.
