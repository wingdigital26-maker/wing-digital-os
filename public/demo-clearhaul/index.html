Questions, answered

Junk removal FAQs

How much does junk removal cost?

We price by how much of our trailer your junk fills, quoted upfront before we load anything. Single items typically run $75 to $150 and a full trailer $500 to $600 (typical ranges). Full breakdown in our DFW pricing guide.

Do you offer same-day junk removal?

Usually yes. Call before noon and a truck can typically be there the same afternoon, 7 days a week across Dallas-Fort Worth.

What do you take?

Almost everything non-hazardous that two people can lift: furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, hot tubs, sheds, yard debris, and brush. If in doubt, send a photo.

What can you not take?

Household hazardous waste: paint, motor oil, gasoline, propane, chemicals, and asbestos. Most DFW cities take these free at hazardous waste centers, and we will point you to your city's program.

Do you donate or recycle what you haul?

Yes. Usable items go to local donation partners (receipts on request), metal and cardboard to recyclers. The landfill is the last resort, not the plan.

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

Not necessarily. For curbside or garage pickups, many customers approve the quote by phone and get before-and-after photos when the job is done.
