ClearHaul Brand Loadout

Nothing on the live site has changed. This is the menu — point at the marks, buttons, voice, and details you want and they get applied everywhere in one pass.

1 — The mark (logo direction)

The truck stays unless you say otherwise. Here are other directions a junk removal brand can own:

Current

The Truck

Clean, literal, safe. Works at every size. What we have today.

Strong option

Lone Star Hauler

Truck inside a Texas star badge. Instantly local, feels like a patch on a work shirt. Great for DFW pride.

The Clean Sweep

A big broom mark. Sells the ending: broom-swept space, not the junk. Pairs with "swept clean" motifs sitewide.

The Second Life Loop

Leaf-and-arrow loop mark. Leads with the donate-and-recycle story. Softest, most modern feel of the set.

The Heavy Lifter

Flexed-arm / muscle mark. Sells "we do 100% of the lifting." Loudest personality, gym-poster energy.

The Raccoon

A friendly masked bandit mascot. Most memorable and meme-able, shows up in intro animations and 404 pages. Boldest choice.

4 — Micro-branding details

Small touches that make it feel designed, not templated. Any combination works:

DonatedRecycled

Rubber stamps

Stamped badges on testimonials and blog images, like inspection stamps on a clipboard.

Load-line motif

The truck-fill meter becomes the site's signature: section dividers, list bullets, scroll progress bar at the top.

Truck crossings

The intro truck (with its speed lines) reappears small: driving across the footer, pulling the mobile call bar in, on the 404 page.

Swept-clean underline

Broom-stroke underlines

Every H2 gets a hand-swept green underline stroke, tying back to the broom-swept promise.

My pick if you want a fast answer: Lone Star Hauler mark + work-boot slab buttons + straight-shooter voice with a dash of playful undertaker + load-line motif and stamps. Tell me your combo and it applies sitewide in one pass. — Demo board by Wing Digital, not linked from the live site.
