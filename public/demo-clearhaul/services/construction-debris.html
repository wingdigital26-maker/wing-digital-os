The short answer

Demo debris, hauled without the dumpster

Heros crews haul every kind of light construction and yard debris across DFW: lumber and trim, drywall, tile and flooring tear-out, cabinets and countertops, fence panels and posts, decking, shingles from small jobs, brush piles, and storm debris. We load it from wherever it landed, the driveway, the backyard, or the garage corner it was "temporarily" stacked in, and sweep the slab after.

How pricing works (no surprises)

  • Standard debris: priced by trailer volume like everything else, quoted before loading starts.
  • Heavy material: concrete, brick, dirt, and tile hit landfill scales hard, so they price as bed-loads. We flag it on sight, honestly, not on the invoice.
  • Full breakdown: see what junk removal costs in DFW.

Junk crew vs renting a dumpster

For a finished project, a crew usually wins: no rental period, no street permit, no loading it yourself, and no metal box killing the lawn for a week. Dumpsters earn their keep on month-long renovations producing debris daily. If the pile already exists, book the crew and it is gone in an afternoon.

What is included in every debris pickup

  • Two-person crew that does all the loading, from anywhere on the property
  • Sorting: clean lumber and metal separated for recycling
  • Sweep-down of the driveway or slab where the pile sat
  • Honest bed-load pricing on heavy material, flagged upfront

Contractors: we run recurring end-of-week pickups for small remodel crews, so your job sites stay clean without a dumpster line item. Mention it when you request a quote.

Frequently asked questions

Do you take concrete, brick, and dirt?

Yes, priced as bed-loads because of landfill weight fees. Flagged and priced upfront, never discovered on the invoice.

Do I need to bag or stack the debris first?

No. We load loose piles as they sit. Stacking speeds loading, which can help a volume-based price, but pointing works.

Can you haul debris from the backyard?

Yes. The crew carries or wheelbarrows it from anywhere on the property as part of the quoted price.

Is junk removal cheaper than a dumpster for a remodel?

For a finished project, usually yes. Month-long renovations producing daily debris are dumpster territory, and we will tell you so.
