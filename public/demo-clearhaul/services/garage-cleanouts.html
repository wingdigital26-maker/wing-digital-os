The short answer

From "we should deal with this" to done in one morning

Every garage in Texas ends up the same way: the car in the driveway and ten years of maybes inside. Heros crews clear single and two-car garages across DFW in one visit. You decide what stays, we do everything else: carrying, loading, sorting donatables out of the pile, and sweeping the slab when it is empty. Prefer to sort first? Our garage cleanout checklist walks you through the four-pile method our crews use.

How pricing works (no surprises)

  • Priced by trailer volume: a typical one-car garage of clutter is about a half trailer; a packed two-car garage can fill it.
  • Quoted before loading: the crew looks at the pile, gives you the number, and nothing moves until you approve it.
  • Heavy material flagged upfront: concrete, dirt, and shingles in the corners get priced honestly on the spot, not discovered on the invoice.

Full local numbers live in our pricing guide: What junk removal costs in DFW.

What is included in every cleanout

  • Two-person crew, dollies, and bins for loose items
  • Point-and-go sorting, or hands-off hauling of a pre-sorted go pile
  • Donation routing for tools, bikes, and gear that still work
  • Metal, cardboard, and e-waste separated for recycling
  • Full sweep-out of the slab before we leave

One heads-up: paint, motor oil, gasoline, propane, and pesticides cannot ride on the truck under Texas disposal rules. Most DFW cities take them free at household hazardous waste centers, and we will point you to your city's program when we quote the job.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a garage cleanout take?

Most take one to three hours with a two-person crew, including the sweep-out. Heavily packed garages run longer, and we give you a realistic window on sight.

Do I need to sort before the crew arrives?

No. Pointing works. Pre-sorting into keep and go areas speeds loading, which can lower a volume-based price.

What garage items can you not take?

Household hazardous waste: paint, oil, antifreeze, gasoline, propane, pesticides. Your city's hazardous waste program takes them, usually free.

How much does a garage cleanout cost in DFW?

Typically $250 to $400 for a one-car garage's worth and up to $500 to $600 for a packed two-car garage (illustrative demo figures), always approved upfront.
