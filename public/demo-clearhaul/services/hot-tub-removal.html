The short answer

The big backyard three, handled in one visit

These are the jobs that stall for years because they feel like projects. They are, just not yours. Hero’s crews cut out hot tubs, tear down sheds, and unbolt playsets across DFW in a single scheduled visit, then load everything and haul it off. You watch from the porch with something cold.

How each one goes

Hot tubs

Drain it the day before and have an electrician kill and cap the circuit. The crew walks it out on dollies if the gate allows, or cuts it into sections right in place, which sounds dramatic and takes about an hour. Typical range: $300 to $600 (typical ranges).

Sheds

Contents cleared, panels unbolted or cut, frame dropped and hauled, all in a morning for a standard 8x10. Metal or wood both work. Typical range: $350 to $800 (typical ranges).

Playsets and trampolines

Unbolted, cut where rusted, metal separated for recycling. Good-condition wood sets can sometimes be rehomed with lead time. Typical range: $150 to $400 (typical ranges).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to drain the hot tub first?

Yes, ideally the day before. We can pump the last few inches, but a full tub cannot be moved or cut safely.

Who disconnects the power?

An electrician kills and caps the circuit first. We take it from there. Electrical work stays with electricians.

Do you remove shed contents too?

Yes, quoted as part of the same load, or clear it yourself first to keep the volume price down.

Can the concrete pad go too?

Pad demo is quoted separately as heavy bed-load material. Ask during the walkthrough.
