The short answer

Green waste and yard piles, hauled without the dumpster

Hero’s crews clear every kind of yard debris across DFW: tree branches and limbs, brush and hedge trimmings, leaves and grass, old fence panels and posts, and storm debris. We load it from wherever it sits, the backyard, the side yard, or the pile by the curb, and haul it straight off.

How pricing works (no surprises)

  • Standard yard debris: priced by trailer volume like everything else, quoted before loading starts.
  • Heavy material: dirt, sod, and rock hit landfill scales hard, so they price as bed-loads. We flag it on sight, honestly, not on the invoice.
  • Full breakdown: see what junk removal costs in DFW.

Junk crew vs renting a dumpster

For a one-time yard cleanup, a crew usually wins: no rental period, no street permit, no loading it yourself, and no metal box killing the lawn for a week. If the pile already exists, book the crew and it is gone in an afternoon.

What is included in every yard pickup

  • Two-person crew that does all the loading, from anywhere on the property
  • Loose piles loaded as they sit, no bagging required
  • Honest bed-load pricing on heavy material like dirt and rock, flagged upfront

Seasonal cleanups: we clear the whole yard after a storm, a big trim, or an end-of-season cleanup in one visit. Mention it when you request a quote.

Frequently asked questions

Do you take branches, brush, and yard waste?

Yes. Branches, brush piles, leaves, grass, and hedge trimmings are all standard loads, loaded loose as they sit and priced by volume.

Do I need to bag or bundle it first?

No. We load loose piles as they sit. Bagging or stacking speeds loading, which can help a volume-based price, but pointing works.

Can you haul debris from the backyard?

Yes. The crew carries or wheelbarrows it from anywhere on the property as part of the quoted price.

Do you take old fencing and storm debris?

Yes. Downed fence panels and posts, fallen limbs, and storm debris are all standard yard loads. Send a photo and we quote it upfront.
