Our story

Meet Mike Fresh

Mike Fresh spent twelve years turning wrenches for other plumbing companies around Dallas. He was good at the work. What he could not stomach was the sales script: the pressure to upsell scared homeowners, the "diagnostic fees" that vanished into thin air, the quotes that grew legs between the driveway and the invoice.

So in 2012 he bought a used van, painted a water drop on the side, and started FreshCo Plumbing with a rule his dad, a career pipefitter, drilled into him: quote it straight, fix it right, and clean up like you were never there.

The first year was Mike, his phone, and a lot of late nights. Word got around. Homeowners in Plano told neighbors in Frisco. A property manager in Fort Worth handed his number to three more. Today FreshCo runs twelve trucks across the metroplex, and every plumber on the team is hired against one question: would Mike send this person to his mom's house?

Still owner-operated. Mike reviews every warranty callback personally. If a job was not done right the first time, he wants to know why, and you get it fixed free.

What we believe

Plumbing is a trust business. You cannot see inside your walls. That means the quote, the diagnosis, and the invoice all run on our honesty. We protect that trust like it is the whole company, because it is.

Flat-rate means flat-rate. You approve a price before we start. If the job turns out harder than expected, that is our problem, not yours.

Local matters. Our plumbers live in the cities they serve. The person unclogging your drain in Garland probably drives past your street on the way to their kid's school.

Licensed, insured, accountable
