The short answer

Same-day hot water, gas or electric, tank or tankless

FreshCo repairs and replaces every common water heater type in DFW homes: 40 and 50 gallon gas and electric tanks, power-vent units, and tankless systems from all major brands. Our trucks carry the most common units and parts, so a morning call usually means hot water by dinner in Dallas, Plano, Frisco, Arlington, and across the metroplex.

Repair or replace? Our honest rule of thumb

  • Under 8 years old, tank not leaking: repair. Thermostats, heating elements, thermocouples, and gas valves are affordable fixes.
  • Tank itself is leaking: replace. A leaking tank cannot be repaired, and it only gets worse.
  • Over 10 years old with any major fault: replace. You would be putting new parts on borrowed time.
  • High gas bills plus a big family: ask us about tankless. Endless hot water and lower operating cost, though the upfront install runs higher.

What is included in every FreshCo install

  • Haul-away and disposal of your old unit
  • New supply lines, gas flex or electrical whip, and pan where required
  • Expansion tank and code-required safety updates
  • City permit and inspection scheduling
  • Written workmanship warranty on top of the manufacturer warranty

Popping or rumbling sounds from your tank? That is sediment buildup, common with hard North Texas water. A flush now can add years to the unit. Mention it when you book a visit.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a water heater replacement take?

Most standard tank swaps take 2 to 3 hours once we arrive. Tankless conversions usually take a full day because of gas line and venting changes.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

Under 8 years old with a part failure: repair. Leaking tank or over 10 years old: replace. We will give you both prices and let you choose, without pressure.

Do you install tankless water heaters in DFW?

Yes, gas and electric, including the gas line upsizing and venting most conversions need. We service all major tankless brands too.

Is a permit required to replace a water heater in Texas?

Most DFW cities require one. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection as part of the job, included in the quoted price.
