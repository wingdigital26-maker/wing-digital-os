1 — The mark (logo direction)

The roof-chevron wordmark is the current mark. See the current picks live on the home page, the about page, and the services hub. Other directions a North Texas roofing brand can own:

Current

Roof-chevron wordmark

Clean, literal roofline over the name. Works at every size. What the preview uses today.

Strong option

Lone Star Roof badge

Roof inside a Texas star. Instantly local, reads like a patch on a work shirt. Great for North Texas pride.

Shield crest

Roof inside a shield or seal. Leans hard on the "fully insured, trusted since 2000" authority angle.

House + chimney monogram

A tidy home silhouette with a chimney tick. Warmer and more residential, good for the homes-first story.

Texas-sun roofline

Roofline under a small Texas sun. Sells durability against North Texas sun and hail. Boldest, most graphic option.

Hard-hat mascot

A friendly hard-hat character wearing a roof. Most memorable and meme-able, shows up on the 404 and in the intro. Boldest personality.
