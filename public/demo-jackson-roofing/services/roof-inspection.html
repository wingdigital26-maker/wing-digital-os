What it is

A straight answer, free of charge

Most roof problems are cheaper and easier to handle when you catch them early. A free roof inspection is the simplest way to do that. Jackson Roofing has inspected North Texas roofs since 2000, and there is no cost and no obligation. We climb up, look it over, and tell you honestly what we see.

You get a clear picture of your roof's condition and a straight recommendation. If everything looks good, we will tell you that too. If we spot something, you can decide whether a roof repair or a full replacement makes more sense, with a clear quote and zero pressure.

What is included

What the inspection covers

  • A careful look at shingles, flashing, valleys and other common trouble spots.
  • A plain-language explanation of what we find, good or bad.
  • A free, no-obligation estimate if any work is needed.
  • Storm damage documentation for an insurance claim when it applies.
  • Certified installers and premium materials standing behind any work you choose.

Questions, answered

Roof inspection FAQs

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. Every inspection is free and no-obligation, and every estimate is free. There is no pressure and no cost to find out where your roof stands.

What happens during the inspection?

We look over the roof, check trouble spots like flashing, valleys and shingles, and then explain what we find in plain language along with a straight recommendation.

How often should I have my roof inspected?

A good rule is once a year and after any major storm. Catching small issues early keeps them from turning into expensive repairs.

What if you find a problem?

We explain your options honestly, whether that is a simple repair or a full replacement, and give you a clear quote. The choice of what to do next is always yours.
