Roof repair in North Texas

Leaks, missing shingles, worn flashing and small storm damage, fixed right so a minor problem stays minor. We find the source, not just the symptom.

What it is

Fix the small things before they get big

A roof rarely fails all at once. It starts with a lifted shingle, a bit of cracked flashing, or a small leak in the attic after a hard rain. Jackson Roofing has repaired North Texas roofs since 2000, and the goal is always the same: track the problem back to its real source and fix it properly, so you are not calling again about the same spot next season.

Repair is often the right first step, and it is usually the more affordable one. If the damage is limited, a clean repair can add years to a roof. If the roof is genuinely worn out, we will say so honestly and walk you through a full roof replacement instead of selling you a patch that will not hold.

Questions, answered

Roof repair FAQs

How soon can you look at my roof?

We aim to get out to North Texas roofs quickly. Reach out through our contact page and we will set up a free inspection to see what the repair involves.

Is a repair enough or do I need a new roof?

It depends on the roof's age and the extent of the damage. Many issues are a straightforward repair. If the roof is near the end of its life, we will tell you honestly and walk through replacement instead.

Do you fix storm and hail damage too?

Yes. We repair storm and hail damage and can document it for an insurance claim, coordinating with your carrier through the process.

Is the repair estimate free?

Yes. Every inspection and estimate is free and no-obligation. We explain what we find and give you a straight price before any work begins.
