Roof replacement in North Texas

When a roof has reached the end of its life, we replace it with premium materials and certified installers, and leave the site clean.

What it is

A new roof, done right the first time

Every roof has a lifespan. After enough North Texas summers and hail seasons, shingles wear out, granules wash away, and repairs stop being worth it. A roof replacement removes the old system down to the deck and rebuilds it new, so your home is protected for decades rather than patched for another season.

Jackson Roofing has replaced roofs across North Texas since 2000. We use premium materials and certified installers, and we treat your property with care from tear-off to the final cleanup. If you are still weighing whether you truly need a full replacement, a free roof inspection gives you an honest answer, and a simple roof repair may be all it takes.

Questions, answered

Roof replacement FAQs

How do I know it is time to replace my roof?

Age, repeated leaks, widespread shingle loss and storm damage all point toward replacement. A free inspection gives you a clear answer on whether a repair will do or a full replacement makes more sense.

What materials do you use?

We install premium materials with certified installers, and walk you through the options that fit your home and budget during the free estimate.

Do you clean up afterward?

Yes. We leave the site clean, including a careful pass for stray nails and debris, because the job is not done until your property is back in order.

Can you help if a storm caused the damage?

Yes. We document storm and hail damage for your claim and assist through the insurance process, coordinating with your carrier.
