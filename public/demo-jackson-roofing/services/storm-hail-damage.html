Storm & hail damage repair in North Texas

North Texas hail is hard on roofs. We assess the damage, document it for your claim, and restore your roof after the storm.

What it is

After the storm, a roofer in your corner

North Texas gets its share of hard weather, and hail is especially rough on shingles. The trouble is that a lot of storm damage does not show from the ground. A roof can look fine while bruised shingles, cracked seals and dinged vents quietly let the next rain in. Jackson Roofing has helped North Texas homeowners through storm seasons since 2000, and it starts with an honest look at what the weather actually did.

We assess the roof, document the damage clearly, and restore it properly. If the storm did light harm, a targeted roof repair handles it. If the hail was severe, a full roof replacement may be the right call, and we will tell you which it is rather than guess.

Questions, answered

Storm & hail FAQs

How do I know if hail damaged my roof?

Hail damage is not always visible from the ground. Bruised or dented shingles, granules in the gutters and dinged vents are common signs. Our guide to the signs helps, and a free inspection confirms it.

Do you help with the insurance claim?

Yes. We document the damage, help you file, and coordinate with your carrier through the process so the claim reflects what actually happened to your roof.

How soon should I get it looked at after a storm?

Sooner is better. Small openings from a storm let water in and can get worse with the next rain. A prompt inspection protects both your roof and your claim.

Will I need a repair or a replacement?

It depends on the severity. Light damage is often a repair, while widespread hail impact can call for a full replacement. We assess honestly and recommend what the roof actually needs.
