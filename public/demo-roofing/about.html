One truck, one rule, fifteen years

How a stubborn roofer and a used Chevy pickup turned into one of DFW's most straightforward roofing companies.

Our story

Meet Dan Calloway

Dan Calloway spent a decade running crews for big production roofers around Dallas. He was good at the work. What he could not stomach was what happened after every hail storm: high-pressure door knockers, quotes that grew legs between the driveway and the invoice, and crews that vanished before the first callback.

So in 2011 he bought a used pickup, painted a mountain peak on the door, and started Summit Ridge Roofing with a rule his granddad, a career carpenter, drilled into him: walk every roof yourself, quote it straight, and leave the yard cleaner than you found it.

The first year was Dan, a ladder, and a lot of long summers. Word got around. Homeowners in Plano told neighbors in Frisco. A property manager in Fort Worth handed his number to three more. Today Summit Ridge runs its own trained crews across the metroplex, and every hire is measured against one question: would Dan put this person on his mom's roof?

Still owner-operated. Dan reviews every warranty callback personally. If a roof was not done right the first time, he wants to know why, and you get it fixed free.

What we believe

Roofing is a trust business. You cannot climb up and check our work. That means the inspection, the quote, and the invoice all run on our honesty. We protect that trust like it is the whole company, because it is.

A quote is a promise. You approve a written, itemized price before the first shingle comes off. If the decking underneath surprises us, we show you photos and talk before anything changes.

Local matters. Our crews live in the cities they serve. The person nailing your ridge cap in Garland probably drives past your street on the way to their kid's school.

Licensed, insured, accountable
