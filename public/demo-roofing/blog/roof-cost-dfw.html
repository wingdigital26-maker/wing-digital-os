What a roof replacement costs in DFW (2026 guide)

Real ranges, what moves the number, and how to compare quotes without getting burned.

Published July 2026 · 7 min read · Summit Ridge Roofing

The short answer: in 2026, most single-family homes in Dallas-Fort Worth run $9,000 to $18,000 for a full architectural shingle roof replacement. Small single-story homes can land under that, large or steep homes above it. Standing seam metal typically runs about double shingle pricing. Ranges shown are typical market figures, not a quote; every roof gets its own written price.

A "square" is 100 square feet of roof surface. A typical 2,000 square foot DFW home carries roughly 25 to 30 squares of roof once pitch and overhangs are counted.

The 6 things that move your price

1. Roof size and pitch

Size is obvious. Pitch is the quiet multiplier: steep roofs are slower and need more safety equipment, which can add 15 to 30 percent to labor.

2. Layers to tear off

If a previous owner shingled over an old layer, both layers come off, and that is more labor and more disposal weight.

3. Decking condition

Rotten decking gets discovered mid tear-off, not before. A fair quote states a per-sheet price for decking replacement upfront so the surprise is small and pre-priced. Ask for it.

4. Material grade

Architectural shingles cost more than 3-tab and last noticeably longer here. Class 4 impact-rated shingles add roughly 10 to 20 percent and take hail dramatically better, which matters in North Texas.

5. Roof complexity

Valleys, dormers, skylights, and chimneys all mean flashing work. A cut-up roof takes more time per square than a simple gable.

6. Ventilation and code updates

Older homes often need ridge vents and updated intake to meet current code and to keep the shingle warranty valid. Good quotes include it; cheap quotes skip it.

How to compare quotes line by line

  • Full tear-off included, or an overlay? (Overlays are cheaper and almost never the right call.)
  • Underlayment type: synthetic, plus ice and water shield in the valleys?
  • New flashing and pipe boots, or reusing the old ones?
  • Decking repair priced per sheet in writing?
  • Permit, inspection, disposal, and magnetic nail sweep included?
  • Workmanship warranty in writing, separate from the shingle manufacturer warranty?

Two quotes $4,000 apart usually differ in those lines, not in profit margin. The cheapest bottom line with the thinnest scope is how DFW homeowners end up paying twice.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a roof replacement cost in Dallas-Fort Worth?

Most single-family homes run $9,000 to $18,000 for architectural shingle in 2026. Larger or steeper homes and premium materials push higher. Metal runs roughly double.

What is a roofing square?

100 square feet of roof surface. Roofers price per square, and a typical 2,000 square foot home carries 25 to 30 squares.

Why do quotes for the same house vary so much?

Scope, usually: tear-off, underlayment, flashing, decking allowance, permits, and cleanup are where quotes quietly differ. Compare line by line.

Are impact-rated shingles worth it in DFW?

Usually yes. They cost roughly 10 to 20 percent more and survive hail that destroys standard shingles, which means fewer repairs and a longer service life.
