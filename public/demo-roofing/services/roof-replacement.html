Repair or replace? Our honest rule of thumb

  • Under 12 years old with localized damage: repair. A few wind-lifted shingles or one bad flashing does not justify a new roof.
  • Widespread hail bruising or granule loss: replace. Bruised shingles fail early, and patching a hail-hit roof piecemeal never holds.
  • Past 15 years with any major fault: replace. You would be putting new patches on borrowed time.
  • Hail-prone neighborhood: ask us about Class 4 impact-rated shingles or metal. They cost more upfront and take the next storm far better.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family homes are torn off and re-roofed in one day. Larger homes, steep pitches, or decking repairs can add a second day.

Should I repair or replace my roof?

Localized damage on a roof under 12 years old: repair. Widespread hail bruising, curling shingles, or a roof past 15 years: replace. We give you both prices and let you choose, without pressure.

What shingles hold up best in North Texas hail?

Class 3 and Class 4 impact-rated architectural shingles take DFW hail best. Metal roofing is another strong option if you want maximum lifespan.

Is a permit required to replace a roof in DFW?

Many DFW cities require one. We pull the permit and schedule any required inspection as part of the job, included in the quoted price.
