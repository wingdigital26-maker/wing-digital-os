Our story

Built on honest quotes and clean work

Chris Jackson started this company with a simple rule: walk every roof yourself, quote it straight, and treat each home like it belongs to family. More than two decades later, that is still how we work.

We are not a storm-chasing outfit that shows up after a hail storm and disappears by fall. We live and work in North DFW, and the roofer who inspects your home is the roofer who stands behind the job.

Whether you need a small repair or a full roof replacement, you get a clear written quote, a respectful crew, and a straight answer about what your home actually needs.
