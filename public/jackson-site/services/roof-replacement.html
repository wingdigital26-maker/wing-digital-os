Roof replacement FAQs

Answers before you commit

How do I know if I need a replacement or just a repair?

Widespread damage, aging shingles near the end of their life, repeated leaks or sagging usually point to replacement. A free inspection with photos gives you a clear answer, and we recommend a repair when a repair is genuinely the better choice.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Many single-family homes in North DFW are completed in a day or two, depending on the size of the roof, the materials and the weather. We give you a realistic timeline in your written quote.

What roofing materials do you install?

We install quality asphalt shingles as well as standing seam and metal shingle systems. We help you choose based on your home, your budget and how North Texas heat and hail affect each option.

Do you offer free inspections before a replacement?

Yes. Every roof replacement starts with a free, no-pressure inspection. We take photos, walk you through what we find, and only quote a replacement when it is the right call.
